## Clone and preview

    git clone --recurse-submodules https://github.com/AmolVKandekar/Amol-Kandekar-Portfolio.git
    cd Amol-Kandekar-Portfolio
    python -m http.server 8765 --bind 127.0.0.1 --directory docs

Open `http://127.0.0.1:8765`. Git submodules pin the project source revisions. GitHub Pages serves the static site from `docs/`, including the HTML timeline, CSS, JavaScript, and the public résumé copy.

A dramatic yet realistic aerial oblique photograph of a newly built expressway sweeping through the dry green ridges of Maharashtra’s Western Ghats. Deep pine-shadow foreground, distant layered plateau and softly glowing pale brass twilight on the horizon, restrained dusky jade and charcoal palette. An important S-bend and precision road construction dominate the right half, fine real road markings and quiet atmospheric scale. Very dark soft terrain and mist on the left 42 percent with seamlessly graded near-olive black edges for an overlaid white headline. One or two minimal extremely faint green surveying contour traces confined to the terrain at far right, like a tasteful architectural overlay, with otherwise realistic photography. Cinematic landscape, realistic grounded scale, crisp foreground, far atmospheric depth. No logos, no typography, no watermark, no recognizable people. A conceptual illustration, no claims of depicting any particular actual road.

**Microprocessor**

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: full-bleed photographic hero for an AI automation engineer portfolio, wide 16:9. A photorealistic, striking architectural macro photograph of a single precision-machined graphite microprocessor module rising over an obsidian black circuit board, in the right third of the frame. The hero module is an intricate layered square made from translucent tinted glass and brushed titanium with a fine warm gold etched contact frame. Elegant emerald green light runs down a few etched circuit traces and illuminates real metal edges. Low three-quarter overhead perspective, tightly controlled black studio light, very subtle dust, physically plausible materials, rich dimensional separation. Deep black negative space across the left half, fading to pure charcoal on the outer edges for overlaying white headline text. A little pale mint light at the far right, subtle amber highlights. Premium industrial editorial photography, realistic optics, legible form, restrained contrast outside the central object. No people, no words, no labels, no logos, no watermark, no website UI.
